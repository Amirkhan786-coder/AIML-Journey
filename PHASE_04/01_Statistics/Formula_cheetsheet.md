## 1. STATISTICS QUICK REVISION FORMULA SHEET

### Central Tendency

Mean:

$$
\boxed{\bar{x}=\frac{\sum x_i}{n}}
$$

Population Mean:

$$
\boxed{\mu=\frac{\sum x_i}{N}}
$$

Median (Odd):

$$
\boxed{x_{\left(\frac{n+1}{2}\right)}}
$$

Median (Even):

$$
\boxed{\frac{x_{(n/2)}+x_{(n/2+1)}}{2}}
$$

### Dispersion

Range:

$$
\boxed{X_{\max}-X_{\min}}
$$

Population Variance:

$$
\boxed{\sigma^2=\frac{\sum(x_i-\mu)^2}{N}}
$$

Sample Variance:

$$
\boxed{s^2=\frac{\sum(x_i-\bar{x})^2}{n-1}}
$$

Population Standard Deviation:

$$
\boxed{\sigma=\sqrt{\frac{\sum(x_i-\mu)^2}{N}}}
$$

Sample Standard Deviation:

$$
\boxed{s=\sqrt{\frac{\sum(x_i-\bar{x})^2}{n-1}}}
$$

### Quartiles and Outliers

Interquartile Range:

$$
\boxed{IQR=Q_3-Q_1}
$$

Lower Bound:

$$
\boxed{LB=Q_1-1.5(IQR)}
$$

Upper Bound:

$$
\boxed{UB=Q_3+1.5(IQR)}
$$

### Relationship

Covariance:

$$
\boxed{Cov(X,Y)=\frac{\sum(x_i-\mu_x)(y_i-\mu_y)}{N}}
$$

Pearson Correlation:

$$
\boxed{r=\frac{Cov(X,Y)}{\sigma_X\sigma_Y}}
$$

### Distribution

Z-Score:

$$
\boxed{Z=\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}}
$$

### Regression Metrics

MAE:

$$
\boxed{MAE=\frac{1}{n}\sum|y_i-\hat{y}_i|}
$$

MSE:

$$
\boxed{MSE=\frac{1}{n}\sum(y_i-\hat{y}_i)^2}
$$

RMSE:

$$
\boxed{RMSE=\sqrt{MSE}}
$$

R-Squared:

$$
\boxed{R^2=1-\frac{SS_{res}}{SS_{tot}}}
$$
